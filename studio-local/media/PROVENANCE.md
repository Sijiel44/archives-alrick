# Illustrations du pilote

Les cinq images ont été générées avec l’outil ImageGen intégré, puis encodées en WebP pour le Studio local. Elles illustrent l’accueil, le bestiaire de démonstration et une proposition de jeu des Archives.

## Les rives dans la brume

[Fichier WebP](entre-rives-accueil.webp)

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset: original wide fantasy landscape painting for the home screen of Les Chroniques d’Entre-Rives, a fictional tabletop RPG. Landscape 3:2. At blue hour a shallow river makes a broad curve through two rocky wooded banks. A modest ancient stone footbridge crosses near the middle-right, with three tiny warm lanterns; haze and a dim outline of stone buildings beyond. Low viewpoint from the near bank, layered depth, deliberate open water in the foreground, no figures. Sophisticated semi-realistic oil painting, subtle brushwork and believable stone and water, dark slate, deep blue charcoal, weathered copper, restrained golden lamplight and ivory mist. Atmospheric, legible silhouettes, elegant adult fantasy, moderate contrast and enough light to read details in a dark interface. No text, lettering, frame, logo or watermark.
```
